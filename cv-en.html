About

6 years in product design. Today at CMA Média (RMC BFM), I design for 17M monthly visitors, drive a multi-brand design system, and shape the place of design within the group's product framework.

The craft as a tool, not a signature

I started in graphic design, advertising, and webdesign back when webdesign still existed as its own discipline, before drifting into product design. Not by logical filiation: by sheer fatigue of a world where the beauty of a deliverable was enough to validate the work, regardless of what it actually produced.

My obsession since that shift is for design to be useful. To answer an identified business stake, a real user frustration, a respected technical constraint. To live in production rather than die in a presentation. A mockup that doesn't stand up in its ecosystem is a mockup that produced nothing.

This logic carried me from pure craft (designing screens at scale) toward the system that makes them possible (design system, framework, governance). Not by trading one for the other, but by holding both hats in parallel — because the maturity of a product is decided exactly at their junction.

Madd, on the side

Since 2019, I run Madd, my freelance practice. It lets me reach markets I don't touch in-house (premium brands, hospitality, retail, packaging) and diversify my experimentation grounds.

It's a space intentionally kept separate from my employed role, with its own identity, its own clients (Maison Albar, Chalet Boréal, TechSpark, Hysope, among others), and its own site: maddlynfuzier.com.
